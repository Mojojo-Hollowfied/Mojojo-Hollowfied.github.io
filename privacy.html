Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Nebula Files (the "app"), an Android file manager developed by Mojojo ("we", "us"), handles your information. The short version: the app works on your device, it has no servers of its own, and we never receive your files, your cloud data or your sign-in tokens.

In brief
  • No developer servers or backend. Your files and tokens stay on your device.
  • Cloud sign-ins (Dropbox, OneDrive) are optional and only let you manage your own files.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and we never sell data.

1. No developer servers

Nebula Files has no backend. We do not operate any server that the app sends data to, and we do not collect, store or have access to your files, file names, folder contents, cloud account data, passwords or tokens. When you use a cloud service, the app talks directly from your device to that provider (Dropbox or Microsoft). When you connect an FTP or SFTP server, the app connects directly to the server you entered.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps some data locally on your phone only:

  • Your settings (language, theme, sort order, favorites and pins).
  • Files you move to the app's Trash, until you empty it or restore them.
  • OAuth tokens for cloud accounts you connect, and connection details for FTP/SFTP servers you add. These are stored encrypted using Android's encrypted storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences, backed by the Android Keystore).

This data never leaves your device except as needed to talk to the service you chose. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Dropbox and OneDrive

Connecting a cloud account is optional. If you sign in, the app uses OAuth, the provider's standard secure sign-in. We never see your password. The provider shows you exactly what access is requested before you agree.

ServiceAccess requestedWhy
Dropbox Access to your Dropbox files, with offline access To browse, open, upload and manage your own Dropbox files, and to stay signed in without asking each time.
Microsoft OneDrive Files.ReadWrite.All, User.Read, offline_access To browse and manage your OneDrive files, show which account is connected, and stay signed in.

Cloud data is fetched only when you open or act on it, is shown to you inside the app, and is not copied anywhere else. Files you download or upload go only to the location you choose.

Tokens are stored locally and encrypted on your device, and are used only to make the requests you initiate. They are never sent to us or to any third party other than the provider that issued them.

Revoking access: tap Sign out & remove for the account in the app to delete its tokens from your device. You can also revoke access at any time from your account settings:

  • Dropbox: dropbox.com/account/connected_apps
  • Microsoft: account.live.com/consent/Manage

4. No analytics, no ads, no selling of data

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no advertising. We do not build profiles, track you across apps or websites, or sell, rent or trade any data. The app needs the internet only to reach the cloud services and servers you choose to connect.

5. Device permissions and why they're needed

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 11 and later): a file manager's core job is to let you browse, search, copy, move, rename, delete, compress and extract files anywhere on your storage, including SD cards and USB drives. Android only allows this with all-files access. You grant it in system settings and can withdraw it at any time.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10 and earlier): the same file-management functions on older Android versions.
  • Internet and network state: to connect to Dropbox, OneDrive and FTP/SFTP servers that you add, and to tell you when you're offline.
  • Install packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES): so you can install APK files you open from the file manager. Android always asks you to confirm each install.

Files are read only on your device to show them to you, play them, edit them or perform the action you request. Nothing is uploaded unless you explicitly upload it to a cloud service or server.

6. Format and wipe tools

The SD card / USB format and wipe tools act only on the storage you select, only after you confirm. No data about your storage is sent anywhere. See the Terms of Service for the data-loss disclaimer.

7. Children

Nebula Files is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app sends no data to us, we hold no personal information to delete. If you have a concern, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Tokens and saved server credentials are encrypted at rest on your device, and connections to Dropbox and Microsoft use HTTPS. Keep your device protected with a screen lock: anyone with access to your unlocked phone can use the apps on it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Significant changes, will also be noted in the app's release notes. We will not use data in new ways without your consent.
